# Secular meaning and values work (ACT-style, no God-language)

Provenance: in-repo gloss file (F021). Plain-English summaries of secular
acceptance and values practices. The retrieval path for users who choose no
religion or neutral framing — support here is fully secular and human.

## Values as compass (not destination)

A value is a direction — like heading west — not a goal to tick off. You never
"finish" being kind, curious, or loyal; you steer by it daily. Counselling
gloss: clients who feel lost ("what is the point?") often chase achievements
that empty on arrival. Ask instead: what kind of person do you want to be in
this situation? One small values-aligned act a day rebuilds a sense of
meaning faster than any insight.

## Acceptance: making room

Painful feelings cannot be argued away; struggling with them often feeds them
(the tug-of-war with the anxiety monster — drop the rope). Counselling gloss:
teach willingness over control — notice the feeling, name it, carry it along
while doing what matters. Paradoxically, feelings fought least fade fastest.

## Defusion: thoughts are sentences, not facts

"I am a failure" is six words produced by a mind, not a verdict on a life.
Techniques: say the thought in a silly voice, prefix "I notice I'm having the
thought that...", thank the mind for its commentary. Counselling gloss: use
lightly and collaboratively — the aim is distance from thoughts, never
mockery of pain.

## Self-compassion in three parts

Mindfulness (this hurts), common humanity (hurt is part of being human, not
proof of defect), kindness (speak to yourself as to a friend). Counselling
gloss: for clients whose inner voice is a prosecutor, self-compassion is the
defence they never had. Practise one sentence of it per day; small doses
compound.

## Meaning without doctrine

Connection, contribution, growth, and savouring ordinary goodness — the
secular sources of a life that feels worth living. No belief required.
Counselling gloss: a "none" or "spiritual-not-religious" client needs this
spelled out: meaning is available without any framework they have rejected.
Ask what already gives their life weight — people, craft, nature, service —
and build the week around more of it.
